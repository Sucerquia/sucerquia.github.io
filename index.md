I'm a physicist working on the multiscale modeling and simulation of
molecular systems. I'm a PhD candidate in Prof. Frauke Gräter's group at the
[Heidelberg Institute for Theoretical Studies (HITS)](https://www.h-its.org)
and the [Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research (MPIP)](https://www.mpip-mainz.mpg.de),
as part of project 8 of [SIMPLAIX](https://www.simplaix.org), and I expect to
complete my PhD in January 2027.

My work brings together quantum chemistry, molecular dynamics, enhanced
sampling and machine learning. During my PhD I developed
[SITH](https://github.com/graeter-group/sith), a quantum-chemical framework
that analyzes how energy is distributed across the degrees of freedom of a
stretched molecule, to predict which bonds a mechanical force destabilizes.
Now, in the final stage of my PhD, I'm using graph neural networks and
generative models to predict electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR) spectra
and connect them to related chemical space, so that experimental data can be
interpreted faster and more accurately. I also actively bring modern tools,
including agentic AI, into my research to accelerate computational workflows.

Before moving to Germany, I studied physics (BSc and MSc) at the
[Universidad de Antioquia](https://www.udea.edu.co) in Medellín, Colombia,
supervised by Prof. Olga López and Dr. Pilar Cossio. My master's thesis used
*ab initio* metadynamics to map the temperature-dependent free-energy
landscape of ultrasmall silver clusters. It received a *Summa Cum Laude*
distinction and an honorable mention in the Shizu and Yu Takeuchi Awards,
which recognize the best master's thesis in Colombia.
