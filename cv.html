Profile

Advanced proficiency in multiscale modeling and simulation, with extensive experience in quantum chemistry, molecular dynamics (MD), enhanced sampling methods, and machine learning applied to molecular systems. During my PhD, I developed a novel computational framework (SITH) to analyze energy distributions across different degrees of freedom in stretched molecules, achieving high accuracy through quantum chemical methods. In the final stage of my PhD, I am pioneering the use of graph neural networks (GNNs) and generative models to predict electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR) spectra and associate them with related chemical space, enabling more efficient and accurate interpretation of experimental data. I also actively integrate modern tools, including agentic AI, to support and accelerate computational research workflows.
